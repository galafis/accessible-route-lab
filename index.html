    02 / FIELD NOTES

    Room to learn.
    Space to question.

    01

    Make constraints visible

    The map separates obstacles, rough surfaces, and the footprint around a moving platform. A wider envelope can turn a possible route into an impossible one.

    02

    Keep the person in control

    Start, stop, step through, and inspect the route. Written directions accompany the visual map. No location, camera, or account is required.

    03

    Build toward the real world

    This is a software research prototype. Physical guidance would require qualified mobility professionals, participant-led design, and separate hardware validation.
